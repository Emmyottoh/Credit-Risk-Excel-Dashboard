# Credit Risk Analysis — Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to examine applicant credit risk, loan default patterns, income distribution, credit score classifications, and employment characteristics.

The project analyzes 500 applicant records and presents key financial risk indicators through an executive dashboard designed to support credit assessment and lending decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Applicants:** 500
- **Default Rate:** 8.4%
- **Average Credit Score:** 656
- **Average Income:** $25,322.87

## Technical Stack & Tools

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and dashboard development
- **Data Aggregation:** Summarizing applicant and credit risk indicators
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Visual reporting and filtering
- **Data Visualization:** Comparative charts and KPI summaries

## Analytical Features

- **Credit Score Distribution:** Examines applicant default patterns across credit score tiers.
- **Income and Education Analysis:** Compares average income and default counts across education levels.
- **Employment and Debt-to-Income Analysis:** Evaluates average DTI ratios across employment categories.
- **Age and Career Analysis:** Explores income and employment tenure across age groups.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Enables exploration of applicant records by education and income categories.

## Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of borrower risk characteristics, helping analysts identify potential default patterns, assess applicant profiles, and communicate credit risk indicators to decision-makers.
